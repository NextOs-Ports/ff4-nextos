Put your Android APK for this game in this folder.

The filename does not matter: the extractor identifies the package by its
contents. Launch the port once and it will extract, validate and commit the
payload, then start the game.

Coloque aqui o APK Android deste jogo. O nome do arquivo nao importa: o
extrator identifica o pacote pelo conteudo. Abra o port uma vez e ele extrai,
valida e publica o payload, e entao inicia o jogo.
